Shops in France sold 2.8% more in September 2026 than a year earlier, measured by volume.

Professional version

In France, retail sales volumes grew 2.8% (provisional) in September 2026 compared with the same month a year earlier (calendar adjusted). In August 2026 it grew 3.1%.
