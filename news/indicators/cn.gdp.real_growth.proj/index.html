Economic growth in China

Detailed version

Name at the source: Real GDP growth, IMF projection · Series code: cn.gdp.real_growth.proj

This is a projection published by the source. It is not a measured figure. The IMF World Economic Outlook database (retrieved 8 November 2026) projects real GDP growth in China of 4.5% for 2026 and 4.0% for 2027.
