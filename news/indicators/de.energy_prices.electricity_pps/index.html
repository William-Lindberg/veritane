Household electricity price, PPS per kWh

Households paid 0.3243 PPS per kWh in January–June 2026, including taxes.

Household electricity price, PPS per kWh
0.3243 PPS per kWh (source: Eurostat, nrg_pc_204 PPS DE, retrieved 7 November 2026)
↓ 6.4 % vs July–December 2025 · January–June 2026

Source: Eurostat ↗

Simple version

Electricity for a household in Germany cost 0.3243 PPS per kWh in January–June 2026, including taxes. PPS is a unit that takes living costs into account.

Professional version

In Germany, the household electricity price for a medium user (2,500–4,999 kWh a year, band DC, all taxes and levies included) was 0.3243 PPS per kWh in January–June 2026, down from 0.3465 PPS per kWh in July–December 2025.

Notes
Average price paid by households using 2,500–4,999 kWh a year, band DC, including all taxes and levies, for the half-year shown. PPS (purchasing power standard) is an artificial currency that removes differences in price levels between countries, so prices can be compared by what they cost in local living terms.
