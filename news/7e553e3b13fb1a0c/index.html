Sweden unemployment falls to 8.6%Sweden: Unemployment rate, September 2026

About 8.6 in every 100 people who can work and want a job do not have one.

Professional version

Item type code: data_update · Template: news-templates/1

What happened

Simple version

In September 2026, 8.6% of people in the labour force in Sweden had no job and were looking for one. In August 2026 the figure was 8.9%.

  • Sweden, Unemployment rate: 8.6% (source: Eurostat, une_rt_m, retrieved 7 November 2026) (September 2026)

Professional version

In Sweden, the unemployment rate (seasonally adjusted) was 8.6% in September 2026, down from 8.9% in August 2026.
