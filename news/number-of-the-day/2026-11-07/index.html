Number of the day

One official figure each day, chosen by a fixed rule from figures published in the last seven days.

Number of the day7 November 2026

Japan prices up 2.2% from a year ago

🇯🇵 Japan2.2%

Prices are 2.2% higher than a year ago. Figure for September 2026.

Source: International Monetary Fund (CPI dataset) ↗What is this?Full history and sources

How the number is picked: every official figure published in the seven days before the date is listed. New releases come before daily rates and prices, widely followed economies before the others, then the topic (inflation, interest rates, jobs, growth, then the rest). Of the top five, the one shown longest ago is picked. Nobody chooses it by hand, and the same date gives the same number every time.
