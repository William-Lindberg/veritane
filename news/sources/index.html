Sources

Every news item and figure comes from one of these publishers. Only official sources are used: central banks, statistics offices, treasuries, regulators and international organisations.

How these items are produced.Software collects them automatically from official sources (central banks, statistics offices, regulators and international organisations) and publishes them without a person reading them first. Headlines are the publisher's own words. Every summary comes from a fixed, neutral sentence template filled with the published numbers. Volunteers review items after publication, and each item is marked when that has happened.
