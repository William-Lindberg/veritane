How this calendar is made

Veritane’s software reads the official calendars of the Office for National Statistics, Eurostat, the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, the Federal Reserve, Statistics Norway, the Bank of Canada, Sveriges Riksbank and Norges Bank several times a day. Meeting dates for the European Central Bank, the Bank of England, the Reserve Bank of Australia, the Bank of Japan and 2027 Federal Reserve meetings are copied by hand from each bank’s page, and the software checks every day that the page still shows them. Publishers change dates sometimes; when they do, the change is shown here.
