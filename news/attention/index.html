Attention tracker

How many listed news outlets reported on each topic, day by day. It measures how much a subject was reported, not how important or how true it is.

What this measures

  • Outlet groups: the number of separate news organisations on our list that reported on the subject that day. Outlets with the same owner count as one group.
  • Articles: the number of articles from those outlets that we found.
  • It counts reporting, not importance, and not whether the reports are correct. Outlets outside our list are not counted.
