🇨🇱 Prices in Chile

Everyday prices for one country at a time, from official sources: inflation, household electricity and gas, world fuel and food prices, and wages.

All countriesChile: all figures and news

Inflation

Prices in Chile were 4.1% higher in September 2026 than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (International Monetary Fund (CPI dataset))More about this figure → CPI inflation

Fuel: oil price (world reference)

This is a world market price in US dollars, the same for every country. Prices in shops also include transport, processing, taxes and the exchange rate.

Brent crude oil, a world reference price, averaged $125.00 a barrel in October 2026, 93.2% more than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (World Bank Commodity Price Data (Pink Sheet))More about this figure → Oil (Brent)

Food: world commodity prices

This is a world market price in US dollars, the same for every country. Prices in shops also include transport, processing, taxes and the exchange rate.

Wheat on world markets averaged $317.00 a tonne in October 2026, 37.3% more than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (World Bank Commodity Price Data (Pink Sheet))More about this figure → Wheat (US hard red winter)

Maize (corn) on world markets averaged $226.50 a tonne in October 2026, 14.3% more than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (World Bank Commodity Price Data (Pink Sheet))More about this figure → Maize (corn, US)

Rice on world markets averaged $489.00 a tonne in October 2026, 37.4% more than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (World Bank Commodity Price Data (Pink Sheet))More about this figure → Rice (Thai, 5% broken)

Minimum wage and wages

A full-time worker on the minimum wage in Chile earned $12,800 a year in 2025, 9.5% more than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (OECD, Real minimum wages)More about this figure → Real minimum wage (OECD, US dollars at 2025 prices and PPPs, per year)

Average pay for full-time employees in Chile was $40,626 a year in 2023, 1.3% more than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (OECD, Average annual wages)More about this figure → Average annual wage (OECD, US dollars at 2025 prices and PPPs)

Average monthly earnings of employees in Chile were $1,024 a month in 2024, 5.1% less than a year earlier.

Check the source ↗ (International Labour Organization, ILOSTAT)More about this figure → Average monthly earnings of employees (ILO, US dollars)
