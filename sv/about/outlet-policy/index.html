Outlet policy

Veritane finds economic news reports automatically. To decide whose headlines we show, we use a published list of news outlets. An outlet is on the list when it meets the criteria below. Its views play no part.

What we show from an outlet

The headline, quoted as published, the outlet’s name, a link to the page and the time our discovery source saw it. We do not show or store article text, summaries, quotes or images, and we do not fetch outlet pages. Full report records are kept for 14 days.

Inclusion criteria

An outlet is listed when all of these hold:

  1. Original news reporting is its main activity: its own newsroom, not an aggregator, portal or re-hosting site.
  2. It is identifiable: a named publisher or owner, a named editor, and an address or company registration.
  3. It publishes corrections, or a corrections policy.
  4. News and opinion are labelled apart.
  5. It covers the economy or business regularly.
  6. Its main activity is not investment tips or recommendations.
  7. It is not on the blocklist, and it has not asked to be removed.

Paywalls are allowed.

Outlet types

Types describe the format of an outlet, not its views. Political leaning is not stored and not used anywhere.

Popular press

Popular and tabloid titles (for example the Daily Mail, the Daily Mirror and Bild) are included and counted in coverage. Their headline is not used as the main headline of a report; another outlet’s headline is shown first when one exists.

Investigative outlets and fact-checkers

Investigative newsrooms and fact-checkers are listed separately. Their work is kept with a story as a signal: an investigation, or a check of a claim that is spreading. They are not counted as one of the independent outlets that must report a story before Veritane writes about it, and their headline is not used as the main headline of a story.

Ownership

Every outlet shows who owns it. Publicly and state-owned outlets carry the same label, “Publicly owned”, whichever state owns them: we do not grade owners. Outlets with the same owner count once in coverage counts.

News agencies

Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, the Financial Times, The Wall Street Journal and The Economist appear on Veritane when their copy is carried by listed outlets, because our discovery source, the GDELT Project, does not index their own sites. A page that carries agency copy is labelled, for example “AP copy”.

Removal requests

Outlets, and people named in a headline, can ask us to stop showing a headline or a link. Open a public issue on GitHub; we act within 7 days, and the link disappears from reports on the next data run. Ask for a link to be removed

Veritane shows headlines with links under the rules above. No lawyer has reviewed this practice yet; we state that here and handle every removal request.
