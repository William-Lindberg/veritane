How events are found

An event starts when listed news outlets from at least three countries, in at least four separate ownership groups, report on a conflict, election, disaster, sanctions or a major political decision, or when our classification marks a story as a major world event.

Later stories join the event when they are about the same places, share key words and continue the same story, within 14 days.

An editor can merge, split, rename or hide events. The tracker states what was reported and when. It does not take sides or say what will happen next.
