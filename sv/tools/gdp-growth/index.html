What this means

At 2 % a year an economy is 1.81× its starting size after 30 years; at 4 % it is 3.24×. That makes economy B 1.79× the size of economy A. A difference of 2 points a year compounds into a 1.79× gap over 30 years.

Per person, growth of 2 % with 1 % more people each year is 0.99% a year; growth of 4 % is 2.97%.

Show the maths
GDP_t = GDP_0 × (1 + g)^t
  at 2 %: 1,000.00 × 1.0200^30 = 1,811.36
  at 4 %: 1,000.00 × 1.0400^30 = 3,243.40
Doubling time = ln 2 / ln(1 + g)        A: 35.0 years   B: 17.7 years
Ratio B/A     = ((1 + gB)/(1 + gA))^t  = 1.7906
Per person    = (1 + g)/(1 + pop) − 1  A: 0.9901%   B: 2.9703%
Growth between two years (CAGR) = (GDP_end / GDP_start)^(1/years) − 1

Antaganden och begränsningar

  • Each growth rate stays the same every year. Real economies grow at different rates from year to year, and some years they shrink.
  • GDP can be in any unit (money, an index, real or nominal); the arithmetic is the same.
  • Growth per person is (1 + GDP growth) ÷ (1 + population growth) − 1. It says nothing about how income is shared between people.
  • Annualised figures assume the same quarterly or monthly rate continues for a full year. They are a way of stating a short-period rate, not a forecast.
  • Historical figures, when shown, are World Bank GDP in constant 2015 US dollars (published outturns, no projections). Growth between two years is the compound annual rate; it hides the ups and downs in between.
