What this means

Your subscriptions cost ¤35.40 a month — ¤424.76 a year, ¤4,247.60 over 10 years. The 1 you marked for cancelling add up to ¤15.49 a month; saved at 5 % that is ¤2,405.32 in 10 years.

Show the maths
Monthly cost = price × (weekly 52÷12 | monthly 1 | every 3 months 1÷3 | yearly 1÷12)
Total per month = Σ monthly costs = ¤35.40
Per year        = 12 × per month  = ¤424.76
Over 10 years   = per month × 12 × years (a trial counts from its end date) = ¤4,247.60
Saved instead   = c × ((1 + i)^n − 1) ÷ i, c = ¤15.49, i = 5 % ÷ 12, n = 120
                = ¤2,405.32   (i = 0: c × n)

Antaganden och begränsningar

  • Prices stay the same for the whole period. A weekly price is converted to a monthly one as price × 52 ÷ 12.
  • A free trial costs nothing until the end date you enter and the full price from then on.
  • The saved-instead figure assumes the money is put aside at the end of every month at the rate you enter, compounded monthly. The rate is an example, not a forecast.
  • Nothing you enter leaves this device: there is no share link, and inputs are saved on this device when you switch on “Remember my inputs”.
