What this means

At a 10 % chance of a ¤300.00 repair, the expected repair cost is ¤30.00; the warranty costs ¤80.00, ¤50.00 more than that. Expected value describes the average, not what happens once; the spread (standard deviation) is how far single outcomes sit from it.

Show the maths
Expected repair cost = p × repair = 0.1000 × ¤300.00 = ¤30.00
Difference = price − expected repair = ¤80.00 − ¤30.00 = ¤50.00
sd = √(p × (1 − p)) × repair = ¤90.00

Antaganden och begränsningar

  • The probabilities and amounts are the numbers you enter. Real probabilities are rarely known exactly.
  • Expected value is the average result over many repetitions of the same choice. A single outcome can be far from it; the standard deviation shows how far.
  • Deductible mode assumes at most one claim a year, of the size entered. Warranty mode assumes at most one covered repair.
  • The value of peace of mind, cash-flow limits and taxes are not included.
