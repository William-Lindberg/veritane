What this means

At 1.0800 your 2,000.00 foreign-currency purchase costs ¤2,160.00; at 1.1200 it costs ¤2,240.00 (+3.70%). Every 0.01 move in the rate changes the cost by about ¤20.00. The chart shows the cost across 15 % either side of the rate now.

Show the maths
margin m = 0.00 % = 0.0000
Cost = amount × rate × (1 + m)
     = 2,000.00 × 1.0800 × 1.0000 = ¤2,160.00
Difference = cost(scenario) − cost(now) = +¤80.00
% change   = cost(scenario) ÷ cost(now) − 1 = +3.70%
Rate for a budget B = B ÷ (amount × (1 + m))

Antaganden och begränsningar

  • The rate you enter, or the ECB reference rate, is applied to the whole amount at once. Real payments are converted at the rate on the day they are processed.
  • The margin is added as a percentage of the converted amount. Fixed fees per payment are not included.
  • ECB reference rates are mid-market rates published once a day for about 30 currencies. A bank, card or transfer provider applies its own rate.
  • The scenario rate is a number you enter, not a forecast.
