Alla verktyg

Spara och växa

  • Ränta på räntaSe hur ett startbelopp och regelbundna insättningar växer över tid med en fast ränta, och hur ränta på ränta skiljer sig från enkel ränta.
  • BuffertsparandeRäkna ut hur många månader av nödvändiga utgifter en buffert täcker, målet för det antal månader du väljer och hur lång tid det tar att bygga upp den.
  • Rule of 72Estimate in your head how long money takes to double at a yearly rate, or the rate needed to double it in a given time, with the exact answer alongside.
  • SparmålRäkna ut hur många månader en månadsinsättning tar för att nå ett sparmål, eller vilken månadsinsättning som når målet till ett valt datum.
  • Investment fees over timeSee how much two yearly investment fees, for example 0.1 % and 1 %, take from the final balance over many years.
  • Net worth trackerAdd up what you own minus what you owe, and follow it over time with dated snapshots that stay in this browser.
  • Retirement plannerWork out the savings target for a chosen withdrawal rate and how many years it takes to reach it, project savings in today’s money, or see how long a pot lasts when money is taken out each year.
  • Spreading risk (diversification)Mix two series of returns and see how much the mix moves compared with each part, depending on how closely the two move together.
  • Regular investing or a lump sumEnter a price path (or pick a sample one) and see how buying in equal instalments compares with buying everything at the first price. No return predictions.
  • Risk and returnSee the range of outcomes a simple model gives for an assumed average return and volatility over a number of years, and the chance of ending below the starting amount.

Lån och skulder

  • Debt payoff planSee how long a list of debts takes to pay off, and the interest it costs, when the smallest balance is paid first (snowball) or the highest rate first (avalanche).
  • Lån- och bolånekalkylatorSe månadsbetalningen, den totala räntan och hela amorteringsplanen för ett lån, och hur extra betalningar ändrar dem.
  • Credit card minimum paymentsSee how long a card balance takes to pay off, and the interest it costs, when the minimum payment is made each month, and how a fixed payment compares.
  • Loan offers compared (APR)Turn the rate, fees and term of two to four loan offers into one comparable yearly cost (APR) and a total cost in money.
  • Buy now, pay later: the true costSee what an instalment plan costs once plan fees and late fees are added, and the yearly rate (APR) that the fee works out to.
  • RänteändringSe vad en höjd eller sänkt ränta gör med månadsbetalningen och den totala räntan på ett lån, och hur betalningen förhåller sig till inkomsten vid högre räntor i ett stresstest.

Vardagsutgifter

  • BudgetplanerareDela upp en månadsinkomst efter skatt i behov, önskemål och sparande med en regel som 50/30/20, och se hur de faktiska utgifterna förhåller sig till den.
  • ValutaomräknareRäkna om ett belopp mellan valutor med Europeiska centralbankens dagliga referenskurser mot euro eller en kurs du anger, och se vad en leverantörs påslag gör med beloppet som tas emot.
  • My own inflationEnter what you spend each month on housing, food, transport, energy and more, and see a personal inflation rate built from official price changes for each category.
  • Scam checkAnswer 12 yes/no questions about a contact or offer and see which common fraud patterns it matches, with links to how each pattern works and where people report scams.
  • Unit price comparisonLine up as many as six pack sizes by price per kilo, litre, metre or item, allow for the share thrown away, and work out when a bulk buy costs less overall.
  • Exchange rates and your purchaseSee how a move in the exchange rate changes the home-currency cost of a foreign purchase, payment or trip budget, including a card or provider margin.
  • Expected valueMultiply each outcome by its probability to see the average result of a choice: a general table, an extended warranty, an insurance deductible choice or a lottery ticket.
  • Is the membership worth it?A yearly membership fee set against the savings and perks it brings, minus any extra spending it leads to, with the number of visits or orders that cover the fee.
  • New or refurbishedThe cost per year of use of a new item and of a refurbished or used one, side by side, including an allowance for repairs and any resale value.
  • Rent or buyThe net cost of owning a home set against the net cost of renting over a chosen number of years, with the renter investing the money a buyer would have tied up.
  • Subscription checkTotal up recurring payments, see what they add up to over the years, and what the ones you mark for cancelling would grow to if the money were saved instead.
  • True cost of owning somethingAdd energy, maintenance, insurance, consumables and loan interest to the price of a car, phone or appliance, take off what it sells for at the end, and see the cost per month and per use.
  • Opportunity cost of a purchaseTranslate a one-off or regular spend into hours of your work and into what the same money would grow to if it were saved at a fixed rate instead.

Inkomst och arbete

  • Hourly pay, salary and real wageConvert pay between hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and yearly amounts, and see what a raise is worth after inflation.
  • Take-home payGo from gross pay to take-home pay with tax and contribution rates you enter, and see the difference between marginal and average tax rates under progressive brackets.

Ekonomin

  • InflationskalkylatorRäkna ut vad ett belopp ett visst år är värt ett annat år, med en inflationstakt du skriver in eller med officiella nivåer i konsumentprisindex (KPI).
  • Mini economy simulatorMove the interest rate, government spending, taxes, oil prices and money growth in a small textbook model and see inflation, unemployment, growth and government debt over 10 simulated years.
  • Economic growth (GDP)See how two growth rates compound into very different economies over decades, what growth means per person, and how quarterly, monthly and annual growth figures convert.
  • Seasonal adjustment demoSee on a sample monthly series how a seasonal pattern is estimated and removed, and why a raw fall from December to January is not the same as a decline.
  • Supply and demandMove straight-line supply and demand curves and see the equilibrium price and quantity, and what a per-unit tax, a price ceiling or a price floor changes.
Fördjupning (9 verktyg)
  • Present value (NPV)Work out what a future amount is worth today at a discount rate, and the net present value, internal rate of return and payback period of a series of cash flows.
  • Small business break-evenSee how many units, or how much revenue, a business needs each month to cover its fixed costs, and what a change in price or cost does to that number.
  • Bond price and yieldSee why a bond’s price falls when market yields rise, work out a price from a yield or a yield from a price, and read duration as a measure of sensitivity.
  • Required return (CAPM)Work out the return the capital asset pricing model (CAPM) gives for an investment, from the risk-free rate, its beta and the expected market return.
  • Company ratiosEnter a few lines from a company’s accounts and see the standard profit, liquidity, debt and return ratios, each with its formula.
  • Return on equity in three parts (DuPont)Split return on equity (ROE) into profit margin, asset turnover and leverage, with an optional five-step version.
  • Return for the risk taken (Sharpe ratio)Work out the Sharpe ratio from an average return, a risk-free rate and volatility, or from a list of periodic returns that the tool annualises.
  • Cost of capital (WACC)Combine the cost of equity and the after-tax cost of debt, weighted by how a company is financed, into one discount rate.
  • Yield curve viewerSee the latest published government yield curves, the spread between a short and a long maturity, and how the curve has changed over the past year.

Inga verktyg matchar filtret.

Utbildning, inte rådgivning. Verktyget använder förenklade formler och de siffror du har skrivit in. Det känner inte till din skattesituation, regler i ditt land eller din personliga situation. Viktiga beslut går att stämma av med en kvalificerad rådgivare eller med konsumentvägledningen där du bor.
