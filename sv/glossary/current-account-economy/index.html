Current account (economy)

Also called: current account balance

A country's balance of trade in goods and services plus income flows and transfers with the rest of the world.

The current account adds the trade balance, income earned abroad minus income paid abroad, and transfers such as remittances. A deficit means a country is spending more abroad than it earns, financed by borrowing or investment from abroad. A deficit is not by itself a sign of trouble.
