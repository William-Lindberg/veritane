Tax avoidance

Legally arranging affairs to pay less tax.

Using tax-free allowances or saving into a pension are legal ways to reduce tax. Aggressive schemes that follow the letter but not the purpose of the law may be challenged by tax authorities. The line between avoidance and evasion is set by each country’s law.
