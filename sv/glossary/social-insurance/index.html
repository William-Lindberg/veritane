Social insurance

Also called: national insurance, social security

Public schemes funded by contributions that pay out on defined events such as unemployment, sickness, old age or parenthood.

Workers and employers pay contributions, often as a percentage of pay, and entitlement depends on the contribution history. Benefits are often linked to previous earnings. Social insurance differs from means-tested benefits, which depend on current income or assets.
