What this means

Each unit contributes ¤15.00 toward fixed costs. Break-even is 334 units a month (¤8,333.33 revenue). With the numbers entered, a 10 % higher price moves break-even to 286 units and a 10 % lower variable cost moves it to 313; the comparison below shows both.

Show the maths
Contribution margin  cm = price − variable cost = ¤25.00 − ¤10.00 = ¤15.00
Margin ratio            = cm ÷ price = 0.6000
Break-even units        = fixed ÷ cm = ¤5,000.00 ÷ ¤15.00 = 333.33 → rounded up 334
Break-even revenue      = fixed ÷ margin ratio = ¤8,333.33
Units for target profit = (fixed + target) ÷ cm, rounded up = 334
Profit at q units       = q × cm − fixed
Margin of safety        = (units now − break-even units) ÷ units now
If cm ≤ 0 there is no break-even point.

Assumptions and limits

  • Price per unit and variable cost per unit stay the same at every sales level.
  • Fixed costs stay the same within the month, whatever the number of units sold.
  • Every unit made is sold. Stock, payment timing and taxes are not included.
  • Break-even units are rounded up to a whole unit.
