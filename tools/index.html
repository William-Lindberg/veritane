Tools

Calculators and simulators that show the maths, not the answer alone. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Saving and growing

  • Compound Interest & Savings Growth

    See how a starting amount plus regular deposits grows over time at a fixed rate, and how compound interest compares with simple interest.

  • Emergency Fund Sizer

    Work out how many months of essential costs a cash cushion covers, the target for the months you choose, and how long it takes to build.

  • Rule of 72

    Estimate in your head how long money takes to double at a yearly rate, or the rate needed to double it in a given time, with the exact answer alongside.

  • Savings Goal: How Long / How Much

    Work out how many months a monthly deposit takes to reach a savings goal, or the monthly deposit that reaches it by a chosen date.

  • Investment Fee Impact

    See how much two yearly investment fees, for example 0.1 % and 1 %, take from the final balance over many years.

  • Net Worth Tracker (local-only)

    Add up what you own minus what you owe, and follow it over time with dated snapshots that stay in this browser.

  • Retirement Projection, Financial Independence and Drawdown

    Work out the savings target for a chosen withdrawal rate and how many years it takes to reach it, project savings in today’s money, or see how long a pot lasts when money is taken out each year.

  • Diversification Demo

    Mix two series of returns and see how much the mix moves compared with each part, depending on how closely the two move together.

  • Regular Investing vs Lump Sum

    Enter a price path (or pick a sample one) and see how buying in equal instalments compares with buying everything at the first price. No return predictions.

  • Risk & Return Explorer

    See the range of outcomes a simple model gives for an assumed average return and volatility over a number of years, and the chance of ending below the starting amount.

Borrowing and debt

  • Debt Payoff: Snowball vs Avalanche

    See how long a list of debts takes to pay off, and the interest it costs, when the smallest balance is paid first (snowball) or the highest rate first (avalanche).

  • Loan & Mortgage Calculator (amortisation)

    See the monthly payment, the total interest and the full repayment schedule of a loan, and how extra payments change them.

  • Minimum payments: time and interest

    See how long a card balance takes to pay off, and the interest it costs, when the minimum payment is made each month, and how a fixed payment compares.

  • APR Comparison

    Turn the rate, fees and term of two to four loan offers into one comparable yearly cost (APR) and a total cost in money.

  • Buy-Now-Pay-Later True Cost

    See what an instalment plan costs once plan fees and late fees are added, and the yearly rate (APR) that the fee works out to.

  • Interest-Rate Change, Affordability and Stress Test

    See what a rate rise or cut does to a monthly loan payment and total interest, and how the payment compares with income at higher stress-test rates.

Everyday spending

  • Budget Planner

    Split a monthly take-home income into needs, wants and savings with a rule such as 50/30/20, and see how actual spending lines up with it.

  • Currency Converter (ECB reference rates)

    Convert an amount between currencies with the European Central Bank’s daily euro reference rates or a rate you enter, and see what a provider’s spread does to the amount received.

  • My Inflation: Your Own Price Change

    Enter what you spend each month on housing, food, transport, energy and more, and see a personal inflation rate built from official price changes for each category.

  • Scam Check (red-flag checklist)

    Answer 12 yes/no questions about a contact or offer and see which common fraud patterns it matches, with links to how each pattern works and where people report scams.

  • Unit Price Comparator

    Line up as many as six pack sizes by price per kilo, litre, metre or item, allow for the share thrown away, and work out when a bulk buy costs less overall.

  • Exchange-Rate Impact on a Purchase or Trip

    See how a move in the exchange rate changes the home-currency cost of a foreign purchase, payment or trip budget, including a card or provider margin.

  • Expected Value

    Multiply each outcome by its probability to see the average result of a choice: a general table, an extended warranty, an insurance deductible choice or a lottery ticket.

  • Is the Membership Worth It?

    A yearly membership fee set against the savings and perks it brings, minus any extra spending it leads to, with the number of visits or orders that cover the fee.

  • New vs Refurbished

    The cost per year of use of a new item and of a refurbished or used one, side by side, including an allowance for repairs and any resale value.

  • Rent vs Buy

    The net cost of owning a home set against the net cost of renting over a chosen number of years, with the renter investing the money a buyer would have tied up.

  • Subscription Audit

    Total up recurring payments, see what they add up to over the years, and what the ones you mark for cancelling would grow to if the money were saved instead.

  • True Cost of Ownership

    Add energy, maintenance, insurance, consumables and loan interest to the price of a car, phone or appliance, take off what it sells for at the end, and see the cost per month and per use.

  • Opportunity Cost of a Purchase

    Translate a one-off or regular spend into hours of your work and into what the same money would grow to if it were saved at a fixed rate instead.

Income and work

  • Hourly, Salary and Real Wage

    Convert pay between hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and yearly amounts, and see what a raise is worth after inflation.

  • Take-Home Estimator and Marginal vs Average Tax

    Go from gross pay to take-home pay with tax and contribution rates you enter, and see the difference between marginal and average tax rates under progressive brackets.

The economy

  • Inflation: What Is Money Worth?

    Work out what an amount of money in one year is worth in another, using an inflation rate you enter or official consumer price index (CPI) levels.

  • Mini Economy Simulator

    Move the interest rate, government spending, taxes, oil prices and money growth in a small textbook model and see inflation, unemployment, growth and government debt over 10 simulated years.

  • GDP Growth Compounding and Growth-Rate Converter

    See how two growth rates compound into very different economies over decades, what growth means per person, and how quarterly, monthly and annual growth figures convert.

  • Seasonal Adjustment Demo

    See on a sample monthly series how a seasonal pattern is estimated and removed, and why a raw fall from December to January is not the same as a decline.

  • Supply & Demand Simulator

    Move straight-line supply and demand curves and see the equilibrium price and quantity, and what a per-unit tax, a price ceiling or a price floor changes.

Professional

  • Present Value & NPV

    Work out what a future amount is worth today at a discount rate, and the net present value, internal rate of return and payback period of a series of cash flows.

  • Small Business Break-Even

    See how many units, or how much revenue, a business needs each month to cover its fixed costs, and what a change in price or cost does to that number.

  • Bond Price vs Yield

    See why a bond’s price falls when market yields rise, work out a price from a yield or a yield from a price, and read duration as a measure of sensitivity.

  • CAPM: Required Return from Beta

    Work out the return the capital asset pricing model (CAPM) gives for an investment, from the risk-free rate, its beta and the expected market return.

  • Company Ratio Explorer

    Enter a few lines from a company’s accounts and see the standard profit, liquidity, debt and return ratios, each with its formula.

  • DuPont Analysis: Return on Equity in Three Parts

    Split return on equity (ROE) into profit margin, asset turnover and leverage, with an optional five-step version.

  • Sharpe Ratio: Return per Unit of Risk

    Work out the Sharpe ratio from an average return, a risk-free rate and volatility, or from a list of periodic returns that the tool annualises.

  • WACC: Weighted Average Cost of Capital

    Combine the cost of equity and the after-tax cost of debt, weighted by how a company is financed, into one discount rate.

  • Yield Curve Viewer

    See the latest published government yield curves, the spread between a short and a long maturity, and how the curve has changed over the past year.
