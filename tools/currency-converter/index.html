Currency converter

Your numbers

The ECB file has no rate for USD, so this conversion needs a rate you enter.

How many USD one EUR buys, from your bank or a published source.

The gap between this rate and the rate a provider gives, as a percentage of the amount.

The ECB publishes reference rates for about 30 currencies (verified 2026-11-06: USD, JPY, CZK, DKK, GBP, HUF, PLN, RON, SEK, CHF, ISK, NOK, TRY, AUD, BRL, CAD, CNY, HKD, IDR, ILS, INR, KRW, MXN, MYR, NZD, PHP, SGD, THB, ZAR, plus EUR itself). Most of the world’s roughly 160 currencies are not in this list; for those, type the rate from your bank or a published source into the manual-rate field. The tool then shows no date or source chip.

What this means

Enter the rate for USD per 1 EUR to see the conversion. A rate from a bank or transfer provider already includes its margin; a mid-market rate from a published source does not.

Show the maths

ECB rates are given as units of each currency per 1 euro: R[EUR] = 1.

Cross rate  = R[to] ÷ R[from]          = —
Inverse     = R[from] ÷ R[to]          = —
Result      = amount × cross rate      = 100.00 × — = —
Received    = result × (1 − spread)    = —

Assumptions and limits

  • ECB reference rates are mid-market rates published once a day on ECB working days. A bank, card or transfer provider applies its own rate and fees.
  • The ECB publishes rates for about 30 currencies. For any other currency the tool needs a rate you type in, for example from your bank or a published source.
  • Cross rates between two non-euro currencies are worked out through the euro: R[to] ÷ R[from].
  • The spread is applied as a percentage of the converted amount. Fixed fees per transfer are not included.
