Representative APR

Also called: typical APR

The rate that at least a set share of accepted applicants receive; others can be offered more.

Where the rule exists, an advert showing a representative APR of 9.9 % (example) means a stated share of accepted applicants get that rate or less. The rest may pay more. The actual rate offered depends on the applicant’s credit file and is known only after applying.
