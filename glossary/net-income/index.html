Net income

Also called: take-home pay, net pay

Income after taxes and deductions; take-home pay.

On a gross salary of ¤3,000 a month with ¤700 of tax and contributions (example), net income is ¤2,300. Budgets are usually built on net income. In company accounts, net income means profit after all costs and taxes.
