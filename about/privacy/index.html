Privacy

Short version: without an account we collect nothing about you. Accounts are optional; with one we store only your e-mail address and the settings you choose to sync.

  • No tracking. No analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no fingerprinting, and no third-party embeds that report back to anyone. Fonts are stored on our own site, so not even a font provider sees your visit.
  • Accounts are optional. Everything works without one. If you create one, we store only what is listed under Optional accounts, and you can download or delete it at any time.
  • No cookies. Your own preferences (light or dark theme, Simple or Detailed depth, currency symbol, chosen country or region, and tool inputs you asked to keep) are stored in your browser’s local storage, on your device, and are not sent anywhere. If you log in to an optional account, the login session is kept there too. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them. The Windows and Android apps store the same preferences in the app’s own storage on the device.
  • Release reminders stay on the device. In the apps, a release reminder is stored in the app’s own storage and shown by the device itself (Android schedules it; the Windows app shows it while it is open). No server sends reminders, and Veritane does not know which ones you set. On the website, “Add to calendar” creates the calendar file in your browser.
  • Breaking-news alerts stay on the device. Your alert settings (which kinds, quiet hours, the “Breaking” bar) are stored on your device. To check for alerts, the Android app downloads the public file data/breaking.json from this website about every 30 minutes in the background (with Android’s own background-task system), and the Windows app every 10 minutes while it is open. The website reads the same file for the “Breaking” bar. There is no push service, no Firebase and no server of ours: every device downloads the same file, and Veritane does not know who gets which alerts.
  • No newsletter sign-up yet. Veritane offers a weekly feed (Week in the economy) that your feed reader downloads from this website. If a newsletter service is added later, this page will name it before any sign-up form appears.

Optional accounts

Accounts are not switched on yet. Until they are, nothing in this section applies, and no page loads any account code.

An account is optional. You can use everything on Veritane without one. You only need one if you want the same settings on several devices.

What we store

  • Your e-mail address, so you can log in, confirm the address and reset your password.
  • Your password, but only as a hash (a one-way code). Nobody, including us, can read the password itself.
  • Your role (reader or admin) and when the account was created.
  • The settings you sync: My Economy feeds, followed stories, saved items, alert settings and your country, with the time each setting last changed.
  • Two-step login, if you set it up (required for admins): the secret key your authenticator app uses.
  • Technical login records the account service needs to work safely: active sessions and when you last logged in. The provider also logs the IP address of login requests to stop abuse.

We store nothing else: no name, no payment details, no history of what you read, and nothing from the Money Manager (which never leaves your device).

Why

Only to log you in and to show the same settings on your devices. The legal basis is that you ask for the service (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). We send e-mail only when you start something yourself: confirming your address, resetting your password or changing your address.

Where

Account data is stored by Supabase (Supabase Inc.) in a data centre in the EU. Supabase is our data processor under its Data Processing Agreement, and the Supabase Privacy Policy applies to the service. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS).

How long, and your rights

  • For as long as you keep the account. When you delete your account, your login and all your settings are deleted at once. Copies in the provider’s routine backups are overwritten within the provider’s normal backup period.
  • Download: on the account page, Download my data gives you a JSON file with everything we store about the account.
  • Change and delete: on the account page you can change your e-mail address and password and delete the account for good.
  • You also have the right to object and to complain to a data protection authority (in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; in Sweden IMY; or the authority where you live).

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data, do not use it for advertising and do not track you. No analytics, no advertising pixels. An admin cannot read readers’ settings: the database lets each reader read only their own. Admin panel actions are logged (who, what, when), but the log never contains keys or readers’ data.

Money Manager

The Money Manager keeps everything you enter (income, expenses, budgets, goals, debts, net worth and subscriptions) in your browser’s or app’s own database on your device (IndexedDB). It is not sent to us or to anyone else: there is no sync, no account and no bank connection. You can export your data to a JSON or CSV file on your own device and import it again.

Because the data exists only on that one device and in that one browser or app, clearing site data, uninstalling the app or losing the device deletes it. An exported file is the only copy outside the device.

Tool links you share

Most calculators have a Copy link button that puts the numbers you typed into the web address, so someone else can open the same calculation. That address is stored in your browser history and is visible to anyone you send it to and to the host that serves the page. Nothing is sent until you share it. Tools that hold personal lists (Net Worth Tracker, Subscription Audit) have no link sharing at all; their data stays on your device until you press Forget, and you can export it to a file instead.

What our host sees

The website is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information to deliver the page, and the GitHub General Privacy Statement applies to that. We do not have access to those logs.

The Windows and Android apps fetch news and data updates from the same GitHub-hosted files (https://william-lindberg.github.io/veritane/data/), so GitHub sees the same kind of request information from the apps. The apps send nothing else and work offline with the data they were shipped with.

Data sources

Tools that show live data (for example exchange rates) read our own static copies of the official data, hosted with the website. Your browser does not contact the original data publishers, and there is no server of our own in between. The only Veritane service that is not a static file is the optional account service below.

Error reports

Error reports and suggestions go to GitHub Issues, which are public and covered by GitHub’s terms. Please leave out personal information.

Children

The site is suitable for young people, and everything works without an account. If you are under the age at which your country lets you agree to online services yourself (13 to 16 in the EU), ask a parent or guardian before you create an account.

Changes and contact

Changes to this page are dated here. Questions go to GitHub Issues; there is no email address yet. See also the Disclaimer.
