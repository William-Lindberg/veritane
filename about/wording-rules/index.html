Wording rules

Veritane describes economic figures and events in neutral words. The list below is the file the software reads when it writes and checks text, so this page and the checks cannot drift apart.

Why

Words such as these add judgement, emotion or a guess about the future to a figure. Veritane reports what the source published and leaves the judgement to the reader.

Words the software checks for

The software checks for these 112 words and phrases in generated news text, in tool text and in the news page text kept in code. Lessons, glossary entries and Protect pages are checked too, except for the words allowed in explanations. Other hand-written pages are not checked, and some quote these words as examples. Words are matched as whole words, in upper or lower case.

A
alarming, analysts say, as expected
B
bad news, bear, bearish, because of, better, blame, bloodbath, boom, bull, bullish, bust
C
carnage, chaos, collapse, collapsed, collapses, could, crash, crashed, crashes, crisis
D
despite, disappointing, dovish, dramatic, driven by, due to
E
expected to, experts say, explode, exploded, explodes
F
fear, finally, fragile
G
good news
H
hawkish, healthy, historic, huge
I
impressive
J
jitters, just
L
likely, looms
M
markets fear, massive, may, meltdown, merely, might
N
nosedive, nosedived
O
only
P
panic, plummet, plummeted, plummets, plunge, plunged, plunges, plunging, poised
R
rally, record, red-hot, risks, robust, rocket, rocketed, rockets
S
set to, shock, shocking, sick, skyrocket, skyrocketed, skyrockets, sluggish, slump, slumped, soar, soared, soaring, soars, spike, spiked, spikes, strong, stunning, surge, surged, surges, surging, surprisingly
T

U

W
weak, worrying, worse

Extra rules for hand-written pages

Extra words checked on hand-written pages and in tool text

gotcha, rip-off, trap, traps, trick, tricks

Phrases that tell the reader what to do

you should, you must, always, never

Words that do not start a sentence, so that pages describe instead of instructing

Automate, Set, Keep, Prefer, Compare, Avoid, Try, Make sure, Do not, Don't

Patterns that say what someone wants from the reader

  • is / are set [up]to keep you
  • designed to make / keep / get / push you
  • want / wants you to spend / borrow / stay

Phrases that need a source link in the same paragraph

historically, most people, on average, more often than not, studies show

Words allowed in explanations

Hand-written explainers can use these words when they state a documented condition. Generated text and tool text are checked for them.

only, just, may, might, could, likely, due to, because of, usually

Other checks

  • No exclamation marks.
  • No emoji.
  • Each sentence in the Simple version of generated text has at most 35 words.
  • Headlines from publishers are quoted word for word and are not checked or changed.

Templates

Generated sentences come from fixed templates. Each generated item records the template version it was made with.

  • news-templates/1
  • alert-templates/1
  • indicator-templates/1
  • revision-templates/1
  • calendar-templates/1
  • digest-templates/1

Example from the current data

Detailed version
In the United Kingdom, CPI inflation (12-month rate) was 3.5% in September 2026, up from 3.1% in August 2026.
Simple version
Prices in the United Kingdom were 3.5% higher in September 2026 than a year earlier. In August 2026 they were 3.1% higher.
