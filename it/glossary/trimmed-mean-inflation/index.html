Trimmed mean inflation

Also called: trimmed mean

An inflation measure that drops the largest price rises and falls before averaging.

A trimmed mean might remove the 15 % of items with the biggest rises and the 15 % with the biggest falls each month (example). The remaining changes are averaged. Unlike exclusion-based core measures (such as consumer prices excluding food and energy), the items removed change each month.

Explainers that use this term

  • CPI, HICP, PCE, Core, Trimmed Mean and Deflators
