Grace period

Also called: interest-free period

The time between a credit card statement and its due date during which paying the full balance avoids interest.

If the statement balance is paid in full by the due date, many cards charge no interest on purchases. Paying less than the full amount usually means interest is charged, sometimes back to the purchase date. Cash withdrawals often have no grace period. The term is also used for a delay before a loan’s repayments start.
