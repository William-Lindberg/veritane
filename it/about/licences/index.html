Licences and attribution

Veritane’s own code and content

© 2026 The Veritane Foundation. All rights reserved. Free to use.

The website and the apps are free for everyone to use and read. The lessons, glossary, Protect pages, news templates, tools, images and software made for Veritane are not released under an open licence: copying, republishing or adapting them needs written permission. Linking to any page is welcome, and short quotations with a link back are fine.

The name “Veritane”, the wordmark and the lens logo identify the project. They can be used to refer to Veritane, but not in a way that suggests Veritane endorses something.

Embedding charts and sharing

Every indicator page has an “Embed this chart” box with a short code for a web page. Charts are free to embed with attribution: keep the “Source: … · Veritane” line that the embed shows, which links to the original publisher and back to Veritane. The content remains © The Veritane Foundation; embedding does not grant a licence to copy, change or resell it.

  • Embedded charts load nothing from other websites and set no cookies. They show the same official figures as the indicator page and update when the site is rebuilt.
  • Each news item, indicator and Number of the day has a share image (1200 × 630 pixels) made from the same figures. These images can be shared with a link to the page they belong to.
  • The figures themselves come from their publishers and keep the publishers’ terms (see Data below).

Third-party material

  • Parts of the banking, credit, investing and consumer-rights lessons are adapted from financial-literacy-framework by sujiyer, under the MIT Licence. Those lessons name the source.
  • The community code of conduct is the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).
  • Chart colours follow the colour-blind-safe palette published by Masataka Okabe and Kei Ito.
  • Outlet headlines about the same event in different languages are grouped with the multilingual sentence model paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 (Apache License 2.0), run with Transformers.js (Apache License 2.0) and ONNX Runtime (MIT Licence) in our data pipeline. Only headlines are processed; the model is not part of the website or the apps.
  • Story photos come only from Wikimedia Commons, under CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA, and keep their own licence: each photo names its author and licence, linked to the licence and to the file’s page on Commons. Veritane never uses photos from news outlets. Stories without a suitable photo show an outline map drawn from Natural Earth (public domain) or a topic icon.
  • Icons and the logo were drawn for Veritane.

Language models (news pipeline)

Our news pipeline uses free language models that run on our own build server, with no AI key. They translate foreign-language headlines into English (shown as “Machine-translated”, with the original headline in Detailed) and help sort stories by topic. They are not part of the website or the apps.

  • Translation: OPUS-MT models by Helsinki-NLP, University of Helsinki (opus-mt-ROMANCE-en, opus-mt-de-en; opus-mt-mul-en and opus-mt-en-it prepared), under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0); the model cards also state Apache-2.0. We use 8-bit ONNX conversions made by Xenova for Transformers.js.
  • Topic checks: mDeBERTa-v3-base-mnli-xnli by Moritz Laurer, MIT licence (based on Microsoft's mDeBERTa-v3, MIT).
  • Runtime: Transformers.js (Apache-2.0) with ONNX Runtime (MIT).

GDELT Project

Report discovery uses data from the GDELT Project (https://www.gdeltproject.org/), free to use with a citation and a link. Outlet headlines shown in “Other reports” belong to the outlets that published them and are quoted with a link. GDELT terms of use · Outlet policy

DBnomics

Some series are read through DBnomics (© Cepremap), which republishes official data unchanged. Its legal terms say the data keeps the licence of the original publisher; the DBnomics compilation itself is under the Open Database License (ODbL). Each such series is labelled “publisher via DBnomics” and links to the publisher: Istat (CC BY 4.0), the Statistics Bureau of Japan (Public Data License 1.0, compatible with CC BY 4.0; Veritane calculates the changes from the published index) and the Reserve Bank of Australia (CC BY 4.0). The figures Veritane takes from DBnomics stay available under those licences (and ODbL 1.0) in our public data files.

Newsroom article reader

When the newsroom drafts a story, it reads the main text of the linked article pages with Mozilla Readability (Apache License 2.0) on linkedom (ISC). These run only in that automated step, are not part of the website or the apps, and the article text is never stored or shown.

FinanceDatabase

Company names, countries, sectors, industries and exchanges on the Companies by country and sector pages, and the company names recognised in news headlines, come from FinanceDatabase by Jeroen Bouma, under the MIT Licence (Copyright (c) 2023 Jeroen Bouma). FinanceDatabase compiles official exchange listing directories, OpenFIGI, SEC EDGAR and community contributions. Veritane uses reference fields only, no prices.
