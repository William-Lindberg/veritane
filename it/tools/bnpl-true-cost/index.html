What this means

With no plan fee and on-time payments the APR is 0 %: the plan changes when you pay, not how much.

For comparison, ¤400.00 on a card at 24.0 % for 6 weeks adds about ¤11.08 of interest if none of it is paid off before the end.

Show the maths

Each instalment = (price + fee) ÷ k = ¤100.00. Total = price + fee + late fee × missed = ¤400.00.

The implied rate per period x is the rate at which the money received and the payments balance out (the internal rate of return):

Cash flows by period: [300.00, -100.00, -100.00, -100.00]
Σ CF_t / (1 + x)^t = 0  → x = 0.0000% per period
APR = x × 26 = 0.00%
APY = (1 + x)^26 − 1 = 0.00%
Card interest ≈ price × APR ÷ 12 × months = ¤400.00 × 24.00 % ÷ 12 × 1.38 = ¤11.08

x is found by bisection between −99 % and 1,000 % per period. Late fees are left out of the rate because the tool does not know when they are charged.

Ipotesi e limiti

  • Every instalment is the same size and is paid on schedule; a missed payment adds the late fee and nothing else.
  • Fortnightly plans use 26 periods a year and monthly plans 12. The implied APR is the rate per period times the number of periods in a year; the APY compounds it.
  • Late fees are shown separately and are not part of the implied APR, because their timing is unknown.
  • The card comparison is simple interest on the full price for the length of the plan. A card balance that is paid down during that time costs less than this figure.
  • Plan terms, fee rules and credit checks differ between providers and countries; the plan agreement sets the actual terms.
